Privacy Policy for PillOrganizer

Effective October 24, 2026

PillOrganizer is designed to keep your medication routine private. This policy explains what the app does — and does not — do with your information.

We collect nothing

PillOrganizer does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data on any server. There is no account, no sign-in, no analytics, and no advertising.

Everything stays on your device

All of the information you enter — medications, doses, schedules, refill information, dose history, and optional photos — is stored locally on your iPhone using Apple's on-device storage. It never leaves your phone unless you back up your phone yourself (for example, via iCloud Backup, which is controlled by you and Apple).

Photos

If you add a photo of a medication or a profile photo for a person, the photo is stored only on your device and is never uploaded anywhere.

Reminders and voice

Medication reminders use Apple's local notification system. They are scheduled and delivered entirely on your device. Voice confirmations use Apple's on-device text-to-speech; nothing you enter is sent to a server to be spoken.

Not medical advice

PillOrganizer is a reminder aid only, not medical advice. Always confirm doses with your pharmacist or doctor.

Children

PillOrganizer does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children, because it collects no data at all.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a revised effective date.
