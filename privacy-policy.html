DivLens — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

DivLens is a dividend portfolio tracker. Your financial data is yours — this policy explains the deliberately short list of what the app does with it.

Data we collect

None. DivLens collects no personal data, creates no accounts, runs no analytics, serves no ads, and uses no third-party tracking SDKs. Every holding, dividend record, and alert you enter is stored only on your device.

Market data requests

To show live quotes, the app requests public market data from Yahoo Finance (query1.finance.yahoo.com / query2.finance.yahoo.com). These requests contain only the ticker symbols you look up — no personal information, no device identifiers. Yahoo's handling of those requests is governed by Yahoo's privacy policy.

Notifications

Price alerts use local notifications scheduled on your device. Alert levels never leave your phone.

Children

DivLens is a general finance utility and is not directed at children under 13. Because no data is collected, there is nothing to retain or delete.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new "last updated" date.

Contact

Questions: open an issue on the DivLens open-source repository or email the developer address listed on the App Store page.
