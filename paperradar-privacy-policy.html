Privacy Policy for PaperRadar

Effective October 21, 2026

PaperRadar is designed to keep your family's paperwork private. This policy explains what the app does — and does not — do with your information.

We collect nothing

PaperRadar does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data on any server. There is no account, no sign-in, no analytics, and no advertising.

Everything stays on your device

All of the information you enter — people, documents, deadlines, notes, and optional profile photos — is stored locally on your iPhone using Apple's on-device storage. It never leaves your phone unless you back up your phone yourself (for example, via iCloud Backup, which is controlled by you and Apple).

Photos

If you add a profile photo for a person, the photo is stored only on your device and is never uploaded anywhere.

Reminders

Deadline reminders use Apple's local notification system. They are scheduled and delivered entirely on your device.

Subscriptions

PaperRadar Pro subscriptions are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not see or handle your payment information. Apple's own privacy policy governs that transaction.

Children

PaperRadar does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children, because it collects no data at all.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a revised effective date.
