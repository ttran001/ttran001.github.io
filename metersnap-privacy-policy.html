Privacy Policy for MeterSnap

Effective October 24, 2026

MeterSnap is designed to keep your health readings private. This policy explains what the app does — and does not — do with your information.

We collect nothing

MeterSnap does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data on any server. There is no account, no sign-in, no analytics, and no advertising.

Everything stays on your device

All of the information you enter — blood pressure and glucose readings, notes, and people — is stored locally on your iPhone using Apple's on-device storage. It never leaves your phone unless you back up your phone yourself (for example, via iCloud Backup, which is controlled by you and Apple).

Photos

If you add a photo of a meter reading or a profile photo for a person, the photo is stored only on your device and is never uploaded anywhere.

Reminders and voice

Reading reminders use Apple's local notification system. They are scheduled and delivered entirely on your device. Voice readback uses Apple's on-device text-to-speech; nothing you enter is sent to a server to be spoken.

PDF reports

If you export a PDF report, it is created on your device and shared only through the share sheet you choose (for example, to your doctor). MeterSnap does not send the report anywhere itself.

Not medical advice

MeterSnap is a log only, not medical advice. It does not diagnose, treat, or interpret readings. Always confirm readings and treatment with your doctor or pharmacist.

Children

MeterSnap does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children, because it collects no data at all.
